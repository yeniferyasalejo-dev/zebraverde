Care
  • Brush gently with a soft brush, always in the direction of the hair.
  • For a small mark, use a clean white eraser or a suede brush, then brush the hair back.
  • If wet, blot with a towel and let it dry naturally. Keep out of the sun.
  • Store in a dry place, in a velvet or cloth bag.
Delivery & assistance

Each pair is finished by hand in our Colombian atelier. After your order, a Client Advisor will confirm the delivery time for your city. Our sizes are European (EU 35–41); if you are between sizes, an advisor will gladly help you choose.
