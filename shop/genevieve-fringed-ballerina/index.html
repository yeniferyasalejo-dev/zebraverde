Care
  • Clean gently with a soft cloth; for a deeper clean, a slightly damp cloth is enough.
  • Nourish every 2–3 months with a leather conditioner to keep it supple.
  • Keep away from strong sun so colour and texture last.
  • If it gets wet, let it air-dry naturally, away from direct heat.
  • Store in a velvet or cloth bag, in a cool, ventilated place.
Delivery & assistance
