VII · Heritage

It began with a pair of pink patent ballet flats.

A childhood gift, outgrown and given away — and a country where brightly coloured shoes were hard to find. Years later, studying in Buenos Aires, our founder spent her nights learning to craft shoes and handbags. In 2009 she returned to Colombia and founded Zebra Verde.

Every step will be yours.
